CalcFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcFlow handles information when you use the CalcFlow mobile application.

CalcFlow is a calculator application that provides standard calculations, calculation history, favorites, and a Smart Calculator that can interpret natural-language calculation requests using an AI service.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

CalcFlow stores certain information locally on your device to provide application features.

  • Calculation history
  • Saved favorite calculations
  • Application preferences and settings

This information is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage and is not intentionally uploaded to a CalcFlow account because CalcFlow does not require an account for its basic features.

2. Smart Calculator and AI Processing

CalcFlow includes a Smart Calculator that allows you to describe a calculation using natural language.

Example:

"I worked 42 hours at $24 per hour with 2 hours of overtime at 1.5x."

When you use the Smart Calculator, the text you enter is transmitted to CalcFlow's backend service through Base44 and processed by an AI service so that your request can be interpreted and a calculation result can be generated.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account numbers, government identification numbers, health information, or other confidential information into the Smart Calculator.

3. Third-Party Services

CalcFlow uses Base44 to provide backend functionality and AI processing for the Smart Calculator.

Base44 may use third-party service providers and AI/LLM providers to provide these services. Information submitted through the Smart Calculator may therefore be processed by these service providers as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

For more information about Base44's privacy and data-processing practices, please review:

  • Base44 Privacy Policy
  • Base44 Data Processing Addendum

4. Accounts

CalcFlow does not require an account for its basic calculator features.

5. Advertising

CalcFlow does not currently display advertisements.

6. Location Information

CalcFlow does not intentionally collect or request your precise location for its calculator functionality.

7. Contacts, Photos and Files

CalcFlow does not intentionally collect your contacts, photos, or personal files for its calculator functionality.

8. Data Retention

Calculation history, favorites, and application settings stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's stored data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove them through available application functionality.

Smart Calculator requests are transmitted to third-party services for processing. The retention of information processed by those services is governed by the applicable service providers' policies and terms.

CalcFlow does not claim a specific retention period for Smart Calculator requests because the applicable third-party service documentation does not establish a fixed retention period for CalcFlow requests.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application and its service providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

CalcFlow is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to CalcFlow, its features, or applicable privacy requirements. Any updated version will be published at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Your Choices

You can choose not to use the Smart Calculator if you do not want your calculation text to be transmitted to the AI service.

You can also delete locally stored calculation history and favorites using the application's available controls or by clearing the application's local data.
